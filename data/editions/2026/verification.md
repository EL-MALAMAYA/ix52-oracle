## Artwork

The product media are deck photographs, not one file per card. The artist gallery does publish a separate PNG per card, plus box, back, and two jokers. Filename prefixes such as `7OH` and `QOD` match the card beside that artist.

Those files are recorded in `assets.js` with `image_rights_status: "unconfirmed"` and `display_permitted: false`. IX:52 does not embed, crop, cache, or export them. The screen shows a card frame with the correct identity and artist, and says the illustration is not reproduced.

Queen of Diamonds remains Nicolo Nimor’s Code:RED card, not a Lemonade substitution. Jack of Spades remains Jappy Agoncillo’s The Sour Knight.
