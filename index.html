About IX:52

What is IX:52?

IX:52 is a 52-day intentional-living practice. It joins the Hermit of tarot’s ninth major arcana, a standard playing-card deck, a short personal ritual, and the Verbesque philosophy of sustainable motion. You draw a physical card, meet the artist who made that face, and choose one action that can survive contact with your actual day.

The 2026 edition uses @runitdecks’ Bicycle Sari-Sari Code:RED playing cards, a collaboration among Filipino artists. IX:52 writes the practice. The artists made the pictures. Those are different kinds of authorship.

Why the Hermit?

The Hermit is an image of reflection, orientation, and chosen solitude. In this edition the Hermit returns. Solitude is for seeing clearly, not for disappearing from care, collaboration, or the people who help you. You come back to the world with attention, credit, and a movement you can keep.

Why 52 cards?

A standard deck has fifty-two faces. The practice draws through them without treating a missed day as a moral failure. Hearts ask about relating, diamonds about stewardship, clubs about making, and spades about discernment. Jokers are credited, and they are not practice days.

What changed in 2026?

The 2025 practice emphasized discipline, consistency, and four verbs: Guard, Forge, Restore, and Ground. That edition remains archived. The 2026 practice asks a quieter question: can an intention become an action that does not spend the person doing it?

Progress is information. Stillness can be a responsible action. Returning matters more than an unbroken streak.

The five movements of Verbesque

Sense. What is true about this moment? Look at the card and at your capacity.

Choose. What movement is worth making? Take the day’s verb and one proportionate action.

Enact. How will intention encounter reality? Speak an intention, then do something small enough to finish.

Restore. What must remain protected? Rest is available even if the task is unfinished.

Renew. What did reality teach? Close the day when you decide it is closed.

How to begin each day

Draw from the physical deck. Select that suit and rank. Look before you read. Pick a capacity: expansive, ordinary, constrained, or recovery. These words describe the day. They do not rank you. Do one action, or the smaller one, or rest. Write if you want. Close the practice when you are ready.

How to recognize the artists

Every card names its maker and links to the profile supplied for that artwork. The Code:RED cards were designed by those artists. @runitdecks produced the deck. Maiba Studio wrote the IX:52 interpretation and this site. IX:52 is an independent practice.

Five standard-card artworks are Code:RED exclusives: Seven of Hearts, King of Hearts, Queen of Diamonds, Queen of Clubs, and Jack of Spades. Queen of Diamonds is Nicolo Nimor. Jack of Spades is Jappy Agoncillo.

Capacity

Expansive, ordinary, constrained, and recovery describe the room you have today. A smaller action still counts. You can stop and come back.

What it means to return

An interruption does not cancel the practice. A day can be recorded, not recorded, a return, a recovery, or a completed reflection. None of those words mean you failed. The practice is complete when you decide to close it.

Spellcraft, plainly

Intention-based spellcraft, in this practice, means lining up attention, choice, resources, and action with a purpose you can name. A card is a prompt, not a command, and not a prediction. Words like intention, invocation, offering, and release always point back to an ordinary act and an ethical limit.
